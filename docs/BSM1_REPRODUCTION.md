Protocol: 100 days closed loop with constant influent and ideal sensors, then one noisy 14 day dry weather run whose final state starts the evaluated run, as in the published protocol (the published run uses 500 days for the first step; the steady state is reached well before 100). Sensor noise and delays follow `sensornoise.mat` and the class A and B0 models. Differences can come from the solver, interpolation of the noise file, and the fixed 15 degree Celsius kinetics of the verified plant model.

Not reproduced here: pumping and mixing energy and sludge production.
