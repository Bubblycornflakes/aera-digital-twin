# Facility data and import contract
Require a versioned asset register with lane topology, measured working volumes, water levels, tank dimensions, recycle/split points and meter locations. Obtain timestamped influent, RAS, WAS and internal-recycle flows (m³/day), MLSS/MLVSS, effluent solids, lab TKN, NH₄-N, NOx-N, COD including filtered fractions, alkalinity, temperature and DO. Record analytical methods and detection limits. Total COD or MLSS alone cannot initialise 13 fractions. Document fractionation experiments, biomass estimates and nitrogen allocations. Compare hydraulic residence time to V/Q and sludge age to solids inventory divided by measured daily solids loss.

For aeration obtain diffuser depth and count, clean-water transfer tests and fouling history, process correction factors, inlet air temperature, standard-volume reference conditions, pump/blower curves by speed, pressure losses, efficiencies, active staging, power meters and tariff periods. Obtain applicable discharge consent, averaging period and sampling point from site staff.

Proposed CSV columns: site,asset,tag,timestamp_with_offset,value,unit,quality,source. Archive raw rows plus checksum. Reject unknown tags/units and ambiguous timestamps; flag duplicate conflicts, gaps, impossible ranges, stuck sensors and sudden shifts. Preserve censored laboratory results. Report alignment, conversions and exclusions. All future historian/OPC adapters produce the same read-only observation records. Site data, permits, calibration certificates and equipment curves have not been supplied.
