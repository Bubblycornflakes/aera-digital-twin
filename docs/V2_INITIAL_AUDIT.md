# V2 entry audit — revision 52ac92d
## Evidence chain
The two HTTP routes at entry are GET /api/reference (annual arithmetic) and POST /api/simulation (educational tank). The React client submits PID gains and two event flags. Scene labels, KPIs and the chart cursor consume the same indexed sample. Airflow in this mode means fractional command. The chart has both full trajectories; time identifies the cursor, not truncation of future samples. Background digesters/buildings and the cutaway are illustrative meshes. Their positions and depicted tank dimensions are independent of solver volume. The biochemical states are spatially uniform.

Benchmark.tsx originally imported frontend/src/bsm1-report.json, with four published comparison values literal in the component. Opening the page performed no numerical calculation. In contrast, python -m aera.bsm1 builds its initial state from raw influent and a separate biomass seed, advances 145 ODEs and computes outlet streams. It imports no test fixtures. The whole-plant regression recomputes that solution, checks 101 published quantities and checks the displayed snapshot against the calculation. Matching rounded UI values alone would be insufficient evidence.

## Model and test inventory
* model.py: two hydraulic/biological balances, fixed reaction capacity, nonlinear illustrative power, minute PID, storms and liquor. test_model.py covers arithmetic, stoichiometry, deterministic trajectories, tolerances, saturation and routes. No site calibration or measured blower curve.
* asm1.py: 13 states, 8 rates and stoichiometric matrix. test_asm1.py checks nitrogen and electron equivalents, tracer washout, substrate switching, and conditional five-reactor results at two tolerances. Parameters correspond to BSM1 at about 15°C. Transfer uses prescribed kLa. Generic variable-temperature kinetics are outside this selection.
* bsm1.py: five coupled CSTRs, mixed raw/recycle inlet, 10-layer nonreactive secondary settler with seven soluble columns plus TSS and benchmark particulate partition. test_bsm1_plant.py checks clarifier conservation, terminal mixed-unit residual, reactor and stream concentrations, blanket and frontend snapshot. Constant influent and open-loop aeration only. No independent dynamic-weather benchmark or plant observations.
* FastAPI, numpy/scipy, pydantic and pytest are sufficient for current science. Frontend React/R3F/Drei/ECharts can render either recorded trajectory. Plot.tsx expects minute-spaced educational records and 24 h; Scene.tsx hardcodes basin identifiers. Reuse requires explicit per-mode labels and clock units.
* README, model/reference registers, industrial roadmap and validation plans document intended data, optimisation and release gates. Plans are not implementation evidence. No measured-data import, calibrated model selection, historian, MPC, audited project storage or independent facility validation exists at this revision.

## Findings before modification
Whole-plant solve lacks input tolerance validation, solver metadata, finite check and cross-method convergence test. Nonnegative kinetic activities and capped settling velocities apply inside the RHS, while integrated states require independent monitoring. Zero initial autotroph concentration in raw feed does not seed a viable population; benchmark startup deliberately seeds biomass. The numerical comparison includes small differences in the published steady-state figures; retain existing acceptance bounds. Annual reference figures, educational blower coefficients and BSM1 geometry describe different scopes. The common application header formerly showed educational clock/status even for a stored benchmark report.
